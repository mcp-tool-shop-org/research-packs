GO FAIR Austria publishes RDM training materials

 

FAIR Data Austria has created and published an Open Educational Resources package for Research Data Management. It includes brief information, videos, quizzes and further links on RDM topics in German and English. More

 

Data Together: Fostering Cooperation Among Open Science Platforms

The four collaborating international data organisations – CODATA, GO FAIR, RDA and WDS, collectively referred to as Data Together – commit to fostering cooperation among Open Science platforms and support CODATA’s Global Open Science Cloud (GOSC) and RDA’s Global Open Research Commons (GORC).
